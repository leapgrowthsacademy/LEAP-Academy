4 weeks, mapped out

Here's exactly what happens, week by week

No filler sessions. Each week has one job — tap a week below to see what you'll actually work on.

Week 01

Identity shift

Before you can act like a professional, you have to see yourself as one. This week rewires how you show up — starting with what's between your ears.

  • Student mindset vs. corporate mindset
  • Self-awareness: knowing your defaults under pressure
  • Responsibility and ownership over your outcomes
  • Naming and breaking your limiting beliefs
Week 02

Communication and confidence

This is the week people notice change in you the most — how you speak, stand, and hold a room, in an interview or in a team meeting.

  • Speaking with clarity, not just volume
  • Overcoming the fear of being watched or judged
  • Body language that backs up what you're saying
  • Interview readiness and group discussion practice
Week 03

Emotional intelligence

Your first appraisal, your first tough manager, your first missed deadline — this week prepares you for the moments that actually test you at work.

  • Handling pressure and stress without shutting down
  • Taking feedback as data, not as a personal attack
  • Reading team dynamics and working across personalities
  • Resolving conflict without burning bridges
Week 04

Discipline, growth and execution

Everything you've built in three weeks means nothing without the habits to sustain it. This week turns insight into routine.

  • Time management that actually survives a busy week
  • Building habits that stick past the program
  • Career clarity — what you actually want next
  • Your personal growth roadmap to take with you
